Frequently asked questions

Can I try Team before paying?

Yes. Every workspace gets a 14-day free trial of Team with no credit card required. When the trial ends, you can upgrade or stay on Starter.

How does per-user billing work?

You pay for each member in your workspace. Guests and viewers are free. If you add people mid-cycle, we charge a prorated amount on your next invoice.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your billing settings. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

We offer 50% off Team for registered nonprofits and accredited schools. Contact our sales team with proof of eligibility.

Where is my data stored?

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit and hosted in the US by default. Enterprise customers can choose EU or APAC data residency.
