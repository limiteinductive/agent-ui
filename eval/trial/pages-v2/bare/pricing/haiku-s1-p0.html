Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated to your billing date. Downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

Do you offer a free trial?

Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial. You do not need a credit card to begin, and you will get a reminder before the trial ends.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your workspace stays readable for 30 days after cancellation. You can export all projects, tasks, and files at any time during that window.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or students?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and accredited educational institutions receive 50% off Pro. Contact our team with proof of status to apply.

How does billing work for guests?

Guests who only view or comment on projects are free and do not count toward your member limit on Pro or Enterprise.
