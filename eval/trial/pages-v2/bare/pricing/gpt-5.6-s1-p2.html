Frequently asked questions

Still deciding? Here are the details teams ask about most.
Can I try Flowbase before paying?

Yes. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with access to the features included in that plan. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can change your plan whenever your team’s needs change. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades begin at the next billing cycle.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Annual billing saves 20% compared with monthly billing. Contact our sales team if you need help choosing the right plan.

Is there a limit on collaborators?

Starter and Team plans support unlimited guests on shared projects. Guests only have access to the projects and tasks you choose to share.

How secure is our project data?

Flowbase encrypts data in transit and at rest, provides role-based access controls, and maintains daily backups for business continuity.
