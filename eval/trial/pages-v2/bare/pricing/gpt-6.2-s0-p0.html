Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to try Team?

No. The Team trial lasts 14 days. At the end, you can subscribe or move to Starter. Starter supports up to five members and three active projects.

Who counts as a billable member?

Anyone with full workspace access counts as a member. Guests invited to view or comment on a specific project are free.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

You can upgrade at any time. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period.
