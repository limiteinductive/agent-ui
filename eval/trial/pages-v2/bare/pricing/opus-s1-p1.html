Frequently asked questions

Can I try Trackwise before paying?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever for up to 5 members, and every paid plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

How does per-user billing work?

You pay for each active member in your workspace. Guests and viewers are free. If you add someone mid-cycle, we prorate the charge on your next invoice.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your workspace settings. Changes take effect immediately, and any unused balance is credited.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

We offer 50% off the Team and Business plans for registered nonprofits and accredited schools. Contact our team with proof of status to apply.

Is my data secure?

Trackwise is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and offers EU or US data residency on the Business plan.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards, plus invoicing and bank transfer for annual Business plans over 25 seats.
