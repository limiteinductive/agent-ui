Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from Billing settings. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial for the Team plan?

Every Team workspace starts with a 14-day free trial with full access. No credit card is required to begin.

How does billing work for guests?

Guests who are invited to view or comment on projects are free. Only members with edit access count toward your per-user price.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or students?

Yes. Verified nonprofits and students receive 50% off the Team plan. Contact support with proof of status to apply.

Where is my data stored?

Data is stored in secure cloud regions in the US and EU. Enterprise customers can choose their region and request custom retention policies.
