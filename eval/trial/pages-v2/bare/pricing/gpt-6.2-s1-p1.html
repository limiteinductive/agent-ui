Frequently asked questions

How does the Team trial work?

Your workspace gets 14 days of Team features without a credit card. At the end, you can subscribe or return to Starter.

Who counts as a paid member?

Anyone with full workspace access counts as a paid member. Guests invited to view or comment on individual projects are free.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. Projects above the new plan’s limit remain available to view and export.

Can I bring in projects from another tool?

Yes. All plans support importing tasks from a CSV, including assignees, due dates, and status.
