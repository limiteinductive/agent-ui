Yes. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with access to all plan features. No credit card is required.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade from your workspace settings at any time. Changes are prorated automatically.

Does Flowspace work for remote teams?

Yes. Shared project views, async updates, workload planning, and integrations help distributed teams stay aligned across time zones.

What happens after my trial ends?

Your workspace becomes read-only until you choose a paid plan. Your projects and history remain available.
