Frequently asked questions

Can I try Flowly before paying?

Yes. Pro and Business include a 14-day free trial with full access to plan features. No credit card is required.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade from your workspace settings at any time. Changes are prorated automatically.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Yes. Eligible nonprofits receive 50% off Pro and Business plans. Contact our team with proof of nonprofit status.

How is billing handled?

Plans are billed monthly or annually. Annual billing includes two months free, and you can download invoices from your account.
