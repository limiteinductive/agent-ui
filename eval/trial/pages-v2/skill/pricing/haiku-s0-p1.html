Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your billing settings. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

Do you offer a free trial?

Every paid plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access. No credit card is required to start.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are billed once per year and save 20% compared to monthly billing. You can switch between monthly and annual billing at any time.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export all projects as CSV or JSON. Your workspace stays readable for 30 days after cancellation before it is permanently deleted.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or schools?

Yes. Verified nonprofits and educational institutions get 50% off the Team plan. Contact sales with your organization details to apply.
