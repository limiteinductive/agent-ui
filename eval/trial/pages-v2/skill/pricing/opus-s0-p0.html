Frequently asked questions

Can I try a paid plan before I buy?

Yes. Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial of all its features. No credit card needed; at the end you can pick a plan or drop back to Starter.

How does per-user billing work?

You pay for each member in your workspace. Guests (clients, contractors) are free on Team and Business. Adding users mid-cycle is prorated automatically.

Can I switch plans or cancel any time?

Upgrade, downgrade or cancel whenever you like from workspace settings. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period, and your data stays intact.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 50% off Team and Business. Contact our team with proof of status to apply.

Where is my data stored?

Data is hosted in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers in the US by default. Business customers can choose EU or Australian data residency.
