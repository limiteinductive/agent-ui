Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Contact our team.
Can I try Taskflow before paying?

Yes. Every paid plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access. No credit card is required to start.
Can I change plans later?

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and unused time is prorated.
How does billing work for guests?

Guests are free on the Team and Enterprise plans. Only full members are billed.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes. Verified nonprofits and schools get 40% off the Team plan. Email hello@taskflow.example to apply.
Where is my data stored?

Data is stored in encrypted form in the US and EU regions. Enterprise customers can choose their region.
