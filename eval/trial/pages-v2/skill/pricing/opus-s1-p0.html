Frequently asked questions

Can I try Tasklane before paying?

Yes. Every Team plan starts with a 14-day free trial with all features unlocked. No credit card is required, and you can drop back to Starter at any time.

How does per-user billing work?

You pay for each member with full access. Guests and viewers are free. When you add someone mid-cycle, we prorate the charge on your next invoice.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Absolutely. Annual billing saves 20% compared to monthly ($15 per user / month). You can switch from your workspace's billing settings at any time.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 50% off the Team plan. Contact our support team with proof of status to apply.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your workspace stays readable for 30 days so you can export projects as CSV or JSON. After that, data is permanently deleted from our servers.
