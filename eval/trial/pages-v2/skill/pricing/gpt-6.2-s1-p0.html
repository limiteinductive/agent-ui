Frequently asked questions

How does per-member billing work?

Team and Business are billed monthly for each member in your workspace. If you add a member mid-month, we prorate the charge for the rest of that billing period.

Can I try Team before I pay?

Yes. The Team trial lasts 14 days and requires no credit card. You can keep your projects on Starter after the trial by archiving any projects above the three-project limit.

What counts as an automation run?

Each time a rule fires, it counts as one run. For example, a rule that assigns a reviewer when a task moves to Review uses one run each time it fires. The allowance resets at the start of your billing period.

When does cancellation take effect?

You can cancel in workspace settings. Your paid features stay available through the end of the current billing period.
