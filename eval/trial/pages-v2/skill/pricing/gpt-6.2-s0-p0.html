Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to start a trial?

No. You get 14 days to try the features in Team or Business. At the end of the trial, choose a paid plan or switch to Starter.

Who counts as a billable member?

Workspace owners, admins, and members count toward your bill. Guests invited to a single project are free.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period. If you have more than three active projects when you switch to Starter, you can choose which three stay active. The rest are archived.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep access to paid features until the end of your billing period. You can export your project data from workspace settings at any time.
