Frequently asked questions

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. Starter is free forever for teams of up to 5 people. You only pay when you need more members or advanced features.

How does the 14-day trial work?

You get full access to Team features with no credit card. At the end of the trial, choose a plan or drop back to Starter — your data stays intact.

Can I switch plans later?

Anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated; downgrades apply at the end of your billing period.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes — eligible nonprofits and schools get 50% off Team. Contact us with proof of status to apply.

How is my data secured?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Trellis is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant, with EU and US data residency on Enterprise.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards. Annual Enterprise plans can also pay by invoice or bank transfer.
