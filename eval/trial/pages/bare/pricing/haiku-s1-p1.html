Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

Do you offer a free trial of Pro?

Every new workspace gets 14 days of Pro free. No credit card is required to start.

How is pricing calculated?

Pro is billed per active user per month. Guests and viewers with read-only access are free.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Annual plans save two months compared with monthly billing. You can switch at any time from billing settings.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export all projects as CSV or JSON. We keep your data for 30 days after cancellation before permanent deletion.
