Frequently asked questions

Still deciding? Here are the details teams ask about most.
Can I try Flowmark before paying?

Yes. Team and Business plans include a 14-day free trial, and no credit card is required. Starter is free forever.
Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or add seats whenever your team changes. Billing adjustments are prorated automatically.
Is my team's data secure?

Flowmark encrypts data in transit and at rest, provides role-based permissions, and maintains daily backups.
Do you offer help moving from another tool?

Yes. Team and Business customers can import projects from common project-management tools, with guided onboarding available.
