Frequently asked questions

What happens when my trial ends?

Choose a paid plan to keep using Team features. Otherwise, your workspace moves to Starter. You can still view your existing projects, but you’ll need to archive any projects above the Starter limit before you can edit them.

Who counts as a paid member?

Each workspace member who can create or edit work uses one paid seat. Guests with view-only access are free.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Can I export my project data?

Yes. Every plan lets you download project tasks as CSV, including assignees, due dates, and statuses.
