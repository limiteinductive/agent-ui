Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your workspace settings. Changes are prorated to the day.

What happens when my Team trial ends?

Your workspace moves to Starter automatically. Nothing is deleted, and you can upgrade whenever you're ready.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

We offer 50% off the Team plan for registered nonprofits and accredited schools. Contact our sales team to apply.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards, plus invoicing and bank transfer for annual Enterprise contracts.

Is my data secure?

Tasklane is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and runs regular third-party penetration tests.
