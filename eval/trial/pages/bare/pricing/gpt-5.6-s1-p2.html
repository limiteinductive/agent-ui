Frequently asked questions

Still curious? Our team is happy to help.
Can I try Flowboard before paying?

Yes. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to the plan's features. No credit card is required.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes are prorated automatically on your next invoice.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Annual billing saves 20% compared with monthly billing, and you can switch billing periods whenever you like.

Is Flowboard secure?

Flowboard encrypts data in transit and at rest, provides role-based access controls, and maintains regular backups.

Can guests join a project?

Yes. Guests can collaborate on shared projects without needing access to your entire workspace.
