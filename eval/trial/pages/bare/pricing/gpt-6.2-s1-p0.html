Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to start a trial?

No. You can try Team for 14 days without a credit card. At the end of the trial, you can choose a paid plan or switch to Starter.

Who counts as a billable member?

Workspace admins and members count toward your paid seats. View-only guests invited to individual projects are free.

Can I switch plans or cancel?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately, and we prorate the charge for the rest of the month. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Can I import an existing project?

Yes. Every plan supports CSV imports for tasks, assignees, due dates, and statuses.
