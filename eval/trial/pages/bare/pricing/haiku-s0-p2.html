Frequently asked questions
Can I try a paid plan before I pay?

Yes. Team includes a 14-day free trial with full access. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime from billing settings.

How does billing work for seats?

You're billed per active user each month. You can add or remove seats at any time, and changes are prorated to the day.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade instantly, or downgrade at the end of your billing cycle. Your data stays intact across plan changes.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or students?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 50% off Team. Email support@taskflow.example with proof of status.

Where is my data stored?

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. You can choose US, EU, or APAC hosting on Enterprise plans.
