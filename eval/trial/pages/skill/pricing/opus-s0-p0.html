Frequently asked questions

Can I try Pro before paying?

Yes. Every new workspace gets a 14-day Pro trial with all features unlocked. No credit card is required, and you'll drop back to Starter automatically if you don't upgrade.

How does per-member billing work?

You pay for each full member in your workspace. Guests (clients, contractors) are free on Pro and Enterprise. Adding members mid-cycle is prorated to the day.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your billing settings at any time. Annual plans are refunded on a prorated basis within the first 30 days.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 50% off Pro. Contact our team with proof of status to apply.

Where is my data stored?

Data is hosted in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers in the US by default. Enterprise customers can choose EU or Australian data residency.
