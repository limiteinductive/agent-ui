Frequently asked questions

How does the Team trial work?

Try every Team feature for 14 days without a credit card. After the trial, choose a paid plan or return to Starter.

Who counts as a billable member?

On paid plans, each active workspace member, including admins, counts as one paid seat. Pending invitations and deactivated accounts are excluded.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately, with the difference prorated for the rest of the month. Downgrades take effect at your next billing date.

What happens to my projects if I downgrade to Starter?

Choose up to three projects to keep active. Relayboard archives the others so you can still read and export them.
