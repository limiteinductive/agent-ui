Frequently asked questions

Can I try Growth before subscribing?

Yes. Every new workspace gets a 14-day Growth trial with no credit card required.
What happens after the free plan limit?

Your workspace stays available. You can remove collaborators or upgrade when you are ready.
Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. Change plans from workspace settings, and we will prorate the difference automatically.
Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Annual billing includes two months free on Growth and Scale plans.
