Frequently asked questions

Can I try Trellis before paying?

Yes. Starter is free forever for up to 5 people, and the Team plan comes with a 14-day trial. You won't need a credit card.
How does per-user billing work?

You're billed for each active member in your workspace. Guests and viewers are always free. If you add people mid-cycle, we prorate the charge.
Can I switch plans later?

Upgrade or downgrade anytime from Settings → Billing. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes. Verified nonprofits and schools get 50% off the Team plan. Contact our sales team to apply.
Is my data secure?

Trellis is SOC 2 Type II certified. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and Enterprise customers can choose EU or US data residency.
