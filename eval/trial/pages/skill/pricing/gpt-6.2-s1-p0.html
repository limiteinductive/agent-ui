Frequently asked questions

How does the Team trial work?

You get 14 days to try all Team features without a credit card. At the end of the trial, choose a paid plan or move to Starter.
Who counts as a paid member?

Every active workspace member, including admins, counts toward your bill. Guests invited to a single project can view and comment for free.
What happens if I downgrade to Starter?

Your tasks and files stay in your workspace. If you have more than three active projects, choose three to keep editable. You can still view the others.
Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Your paid features stay available through the end of your current billing period.
