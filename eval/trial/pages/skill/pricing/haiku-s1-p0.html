Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your billing settings. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Every paid plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to features. No credit card is required to start.

How does billing work for annual plans?

Annual plans are billed once per year at a 20% discount compared to monthly billing. We accept all major credit cards and invoice payment for Business plans.

Can I add guests who are not on my team?

Guests can view and comment on specific projects without using a paid seat. Guest access is included in the Team and Business plans.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export all of your projects and files at any time. After cancellation, your workspace stays read-only for 30 days before it is permanently deleted.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or schools?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and accredited educational institutions get 50% off the Team plan. Contact our sales team with proof of status to apply.
